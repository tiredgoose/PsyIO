How to play

A shuffled 52-card deck is dealt into nine stacks. The top card of each stack is face up.

For any stack, guess whether the card underneath is Higher, Lower, or the Same rank as the face-up card. Aces are high (2 is lowest, A is highest). Suits don't matter.

  • Right: the face-up card goes to the completed pile and the next card flips up.
  • Wrong: that whole stack is blacked out.
  • A stack is cleared when only its last card remains.

Scoring

  • Higher / Lower correct: 10 × multiplier
  • Same correct: 50 × multiplier
  • Every 3 correct in a row raises the multiplier by 1 (max ×5). A wrong guess resets it.
  • Clearing a stack: +25 bonus
  • Clearing all nine stacks: +250 bonus

The game ends when every stack is either cleared or blacked out.
